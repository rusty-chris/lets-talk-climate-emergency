Dear National Emergency Briefing team,

First, thank you for what you have built — the Westminster briefing, the open letter, and *The People's Emergency Briefing* screenings are, to my mind, the most serious attempt yet to close the gap between what the science says and what the public has actually been told.

I am writing to introduce a project that exists because of that same gap, and to offer a conversation. I am Chris McWilliams; through my consultancy Rusty Data I am building and stewarding ***Let's Talk About the Climate Emergency*** — a free, open-source, non-commercial chatbot that gives anyone the emergency briefing they have never had: plain answers grounded strictly in the authoritative literature (IPCC, UNEP, national assessments, the scientists' warning papers), with every claim cited, the source text one click away, and shareable source-stamped charts from canonical datasets. No ads, nothing sold, code and evaluation results public. It is designed for exactly the person your campaign is trying to reach: the neighbour who has never had the situation laid out for them — a self-serve companion to the televised briefing you are calling for, for the days until that briefing happens.

Two things you should know about how the project relates to your campaign:

1. **We align with your call, and say so — but we claim no affiliation.** The product includes a clearly-labelled "Voices & action" layer that describes the campaign in our own words — the open letter, the petition and Early Day Motion, the Westminster briefing, the film and community screenings — and sends users to nebriefing.org to watch, sign, and host screenings. Campaign material is never cited as scientific evidence and we never imply endorsement; if anything in how we describe the campaign is wrong or unwelcome, tell us and we will fix it immediately.
2. **We would welcome a conversation, with no obligation on you.** If it were ever useful for the campaign to point to a tool like this — as a resource listing, a link for screening audiences who ask "where can I dig into the evidence?", or something more like a partnership — I would be glad to explore that on your terms. Equally, if you would simply like to review how we present the campaign before we launch, the door is open.

No response is needed for us to proceed respectfully — we assume nothing — but I would be delighted to talk. I am UK-based and happy to speak by call or meet whenever suits, including after the August rush.

With admiration and thanks,

Chris McWilliams
Rusty Data — steward, *Let's Talk About the Climate Emergency*
