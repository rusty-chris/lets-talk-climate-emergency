## 1. IPCC Secretariat (`01-ipcc.md`)

- **Addressee:** The Secretary of the IPCC, IPCC Secretariat
- **Email:** ipcc-sec@wmo.int
- **Postal:** IPCC Secretariat, World Meteorological Organization, 7bis Avenue de la Paix, P.O. Box 2300, CH-1211 Geneva 2, Switzerland
- **Procedure (stated on ipcc.ch/copyright):** email a **scanned copy of a letter on official letterhead addressed to the Secretary of the IPCC** with all relevant details (name, organisation, contact details, website, and the purpose of use). Responses may take up to four weeks.
- **Verified on:** https://www.ipcc.ch/copyright/ — page content confirmed via search-engine excerpts of that URL; the page itself returned **HTTP 403 to automated fetch** on 2026-08-16, so the details above come from indexed copies of the page, corroborated by https://www.ipcc.ch/about/contact/ (same postal address and ipcc-sec@wmo.int).
- **Confidence:** medium-high. Details are consistent across sources, but Chris should open ipcc.ch/copyright in a browser before sending to confirm the procedure has not changed.

## 2. Oxford University Press — BioScience permissions (`02-oup-bioscience.md`)

- **Addressee:** Chairperson, Publications Board, World Meteorological Organization
