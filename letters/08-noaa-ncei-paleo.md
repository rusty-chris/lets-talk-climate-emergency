Dear NCEI Paleoclimatology team,

I run a free, non-commercial public-benefit educational website that explains
the assessed climate science to a general audience. One chart on the site — a
"CO₂ and global temperature over the last 10,000 years" figure — is built from
two datasets archived in your World Data Service for Paleoclimatology:

1. **Antarctic Ice Cores Revised 800 kyr CO₂ composite** — NCEI study 17975,
   Bereiter et al. 2015 (*Geophys. Res. Lett.*, doi:10.1002/2014GL061957).
2. **Global Holocene Mean Surface Temperature (Temp12k)** — NCEI study 29712,
   dataset DOI 10.25921/vzys-1280, Kaufman et al. 2020 (*Sci. Data* 7:201,
   doi:10.1038/s41597-020-0530-7, published CC BY 4.0).

We download both directly from your archive and credit them fully wherever the
chart appears (dataset title, study number, citation, and the contributing
authors). We do not modify the data values; the chart splices the ice-core /
reconstruction record to the modern instrumental record (Mauna Loa CO₂,
GISTEMP) with the splice year and method disclosed on the figure.

Your archive metadata for both studies carries a citation-only use constraint
and no explicit licence grant. **Could you confirm that redistribution and
public display of these datasets, with attribution, is permitted** — or point
us to the applicable licence/terms? We are happy to adjust our attribution
wording to whatever form you prefer.

Thank you for maintaining these records and for your time.
