## Context
DESIGN §7.3, §4 item 7/11, §9 privacy. The credibility furniture: what the corpus is, what's excluded and why, guaranteed vs measured with live numbers, the non-commercial commitment.

## Scope
- `/about`: corpus tiers + versions, exclusions with reasons ("Ripple et al.: permission requested"), how citations work, guaranteed-vs-measured (Appendix B text) with current eval numbers (wired from #21's published results), licence/attribution table, non-commercial statement, known limitations.
- Voices & action page from `voices/voices.yaml`: campaign links, film, petition, screenings, experts — with `as_of` dates.
- Privacy page per §9: what's logged, why, retention, IP handling, lawful basis.
- Likelihood-scale legend; footer verification note + corpus vintage; non-affiliation disclaimer on every source surface.
- **Steward credit (DESIGN §7.3, owner decision 2026-08-16):** small "Built by Rusty Data" credit with logo in the footer (or slim banner), always paired with the explicit "free, open-source, non-commercial project" note — the two never appear separately.

## Acceptance criteria
- `test_every_required_attribution_string_appears` passes, driven from the manifests (C3S exact string, OGL statement, dataset citations) so a new source cannot be forgotten.
- `test_about_renders_latest_eval_results` passes; a missing RESULTS.md fails the build rather than rendering blanks.

## TDD plan
Pages render from manifests and committed results, so the tests are data-to-page assertions. All unit unless noted; order:
1. `test_every_required_attribution_string_appears` — for each manifest entry with a required attribution (C3S exact string, OGL statement, dataset citations), that exact string appears on the rendered pages; driven from the manifests so a new source cannot be forgotten.
2. `test_about_includes_guaranteed_vs_measured_text` — Appendix B text present.
3. `test_about_renders_latest_eval_results` — given a fixture `RESULTS.md`, the numbers appear; a missing results file fails the build rather than rendering blanks.
4. `test_about_lists_exclusions_with_reasons` — includes the "Ripple et al.: permission requested" line.
5. `test_nonaffiliation_disclaimer_on_every_source_surface` — /about, source library and voices page all carry the §4 disclaimer.
6. `test_noncommercial_statement_present`.
6b. `test_steward_credit_paired_with_noncommercial_note` — wherever the "Built by Rusty Data" credit renders, the free/open-source/non-commercial note renders adjacent to it.
7. `test_voices_page_renders_as_of_dates` — every snapshot fact displays its date.
8. `test_privacy_page_states_required_elements` — retention periods (90-day logs, ≤7-day IP hashes), lawful basis, and the disclosure line present.
9. `test_pages_served` (smoke) — /about, voices and privacy pages reachable in the composed stack.
