## Context
DESIGN §3.3 runtime/eval-time split. Entailment is measured, not guaranteed; at runtime we flag, we don't regenerate.

## Scope
- After generation completes: one batched Haiku call checking every factual-sentence → cited-block pair for entailment.
- Sentences failing entailment get an "unverified" badge applied to their citation chips after the stream finishes (UI contract with #18).
- Aggregate rate logged per exchange (feeds the published citation-support metric).
- Cost within the §9 model (~$0.003/query).

## Acceptance criteria
- Deliberately corrupted answer fixture → correct sentences flagged.
- No user-visible latency before streaming starts; badges apply within ~2s of stream end.
